# Idea backlog

Ideas that cleared the reversal test but not the data test, or that are waiting
for their moment in the discourse. See `strategy/30-day-plan.md` for the seeded
bank of 30.

Format:

```
## Title
**Belief:** what's on trial
**Reversal:** the one-sentence turn, if the data breaks right
**Data:** what's needed, and whether you have it
**Trigger:** what event brings this back into the conversation
```
